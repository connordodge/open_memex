Place a note on a canvas at (x, y)

Usage: memex canvas add [OPTIONS] --vault <VAULT> --canvas-id <CANVAS_ID> --note-id <NOTE_ID>

Options:
      --vault <VAULT>          
      --canvas-id <CANVAS_ID>  
      --note-id <NOTE_ID>      
      --x <X>                  [default: 0.0]
      --y <Y>                  [default: 0.0]
  -h, --help                   Print help
