Rename a canvas

Usage: memex canvas rename --vault <VAULT> --canvas-id <CANVAS_ID> --title <TITLE> --expected-version <EXPECTED_VERSION>

Options:
      --vault <VAULT>                        
      --canvas-id <CANVAS_ID>                
      --title <TITLE>                        
      --expected-version <EXPECTED_VERSION>  
  -h, --help                                 Print help
