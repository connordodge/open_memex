Move a placed node to new coordinates

Usage: memex canvas move [OPTIONS] --vault <VAULT> --canvas-id <CANVAS_ID> --note-id <NOTE_ID> --x <X> --y <Y>

Options:
      --vault <VAULT>          
      --canvas-id <CANVAS_ID>  
      --note-id <NOTE_ID>      
      --x <X>                  
      --y <Y>                  
      --z <Z>                  
  -h, --help                   Print help
