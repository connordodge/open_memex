## Highlights

Open Memex 0.6.0 makes long notes easier to move around in. The Command bar now works, you can find text in the open note, every note gets an Outline of its headings, and the caret stays where you type or click in headings.

### What's new since v0.5.0

- **Command palette.** Click the Command bar (`search · ask · run`) or press ⌘K anywhere in the workspace. One input finds app commands by name, notes and canvases by title, and Passages of note text by keyword and by meaning. Choosing a Passage opens its note scrolled to that passage, with a brief highlight. The preview pane shows the selected result when the window is wide enough. With an empty input, the palette lists your recent notes.
- **Meaning search.** Passages use the same local model as Echoes. If the vault doesn't have the model yet, the palette shows keyword matches and offers to download it (local vaults). Hosted vaults search through the vault server.
- **Find in note.** ⌘F opens the Find bar over the open note, in live preview and in Source mode. Matches are highlighted and the counter shows where you are; Enter and ⌘G step forward, Shift+Enter and ⇧⌘G step back, and ⌘E uses the selection. Matches inside excerpt cards and tables count, and a match inside hidden markup, such as a link address, reveals that line when you step to it. Edit ▸ Find in the menu bar now works too.
- **Outline.** A new Outline tab in the right panel lists the open note's headings, indented by level. Click a heading to jump to it; the current section follows as you scroll. ⌘⇧O opens the Outline from anywhere, and the arrow keys, Enter and Esc work in the list.
- **Steadier caret in headings.** Typing `#` and spaces in a heading moves the caret at once, and clicking a heading, a bold line or a link line keeps the caret where you clicked when the markup appears.
- **`memex search`.** The CLI has a new `memex search` command, and the vault server a matching search route, both used by the Command palette.

## Upgrade

Self-update offers 0.6.0 to 0.5.0 installs; earlier builds install it by hand from this Release. No vault changes. Vaults made by earlier builds at schema V8 open as they are. A 0.6.0 app connected to an older vault server shows Passages as needing a server update; everything else keeps working.

## Requirements

Apple silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later.
