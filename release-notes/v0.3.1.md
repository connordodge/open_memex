## Highlights

Open Memex 0.3.1 fixes Learn Spelling and other edits failing on a Mac where an older `memex` binary had been picked once with "locate memex binary…". The app now always uses the memex bundled with it, which matches its vault schema. A picked binary applies only to builds with no bundled memex.

## Upgrade

No vault changes. Vaults made by earlier builds at schema V7 open as they are.

## Requirements

Apple silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later.
