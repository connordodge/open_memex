## Box (Key `find-bar`)
- Position: 10 px below the toolbar hairline (hairline is 1 px at y 44, body starts y 45, box top y 55), right edge
  12 px from the body's right edge. It floats: nothing below it moves (no layout space).
- Width `min(372, bodyWidth - 24)`; height 38. Fill `#181D23` surface3. Border 1 px inside `line2` (`#FFFCF0` @ 10%, alpha 25).
  Radius 6. Shadow x0 y8 blur24 spread0 `#0000008C` (`OmColors.menuShadow`).
- Row, align centre, padding L/R 6 (7 from the outer edge with the 1 px border), gap 4 between children.

## Shrink to fit (B4.1-B4.5; `box = min(372, body - 24)`)
| Body | Box | Counter | Counter slot | Magnifier | Field |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 532 | 372 | full `2 of 12` | 72 (min 64 + 8) | shown | 198 (box - 174) |
| 324 | 300 | full (last width with it) | 72 | shown | 126 (box - 174; gave up 72) |
| 264 | 240 | short `2/12` | 42 (min 34 + 8) | shown | 96 (box - 144), the 96 px floor |
| 192 (agents panel open) | 168 | hidden | 0 | dropped | 70 (box - 98), below the floor |

- Rules: field flexes first, never under 96 while the counter shows; box under 300 uses the short form; box under 240
  hides the counter and drops the magnifier; ↑ ↓ × always show. A short form over 34 px (6+ chars, e.g. `1/2,431`) would push
  the field under 96, so the counter hides instead. Hidden or not, the field's semantics value stays the full form.
- At 240 and 168 with no matches: `None` (240) or nothing shown (168), danger field border. No-match counter at 240 is `None`.
- 168 box: query room 52 px (about 7 characters), query clips on the left (`hydration` at x 3).
- Box edge insets are constant: right 12, top 10, at every width (B4.4 box left 52 = 40 + 192 - 12 - 168).

## The design wins: mismatches with the WI body
- Board numbers. WI lists "B3.3 matches, B3.5 no matches, B3.7 long query". In the file B3.3 is the **no matches** state,
  B3.5 is **Source mode** (`3 of 11`), B3.6 is the long query, B3.7 is the large count (`1 of 2,431`).
- The golden "B3.3 and B4.3" therefore compares a no-matches box (372, `No matches`) and the 240 box (`2/12`). For a full
  matching form use B4.1 (372, `2 of 12`) or B3.8.
- WI says "nothing shows for an empty query": the design also keeps the counter slot's 80 px reserved, so the field stays 198.
- WI does not give the unfocused border (line3), the selected-query fill, counter min widths (64/34) or tooltip metrics: above.
