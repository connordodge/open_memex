## Placement (B1, B2, every board)
- Layout of the editor pane: toolbar 44 high, fill `#0B0E11` surface1, padding L/R 24; 1 px hairline `#FFFCF00F` line1 at y 44;
  body from y 45; a second hairline and the 26 px status bar (surface1) below the body.
- The box is a floating layer over the body (`Stack` slot `_bodyOverlays`), **10 px below the hairline** (box top y 55 = body top
  + 10) and **12 px from the body's right edge** (B1: 1120 - 12 - 372 = box left 736; B3/F boards: 532 - 12 - 372 = 148).
  It never takes layout space ("Nothing below moves", B3.9 note). Text slides under it when scrolled (B2: the box is opaque,
  the scrolled title and first line are hidden behind it).
- Body text column: B1 x 180, width 760, top padding 28 (the box overlaps nothing at 1120). B2 / B3 boards: x 28, width 476,
  top padding 10 to 16 by board.

## Scroll-into-view inset (B3.4)
- Body top at y 0, box 10 to 48, **guide at 60**: `RevealWithin(60)` = 10 (box top) + 38 (box height) + 12 (clearance). The 12 px
  clearance is drawn as an info `#7AA2FF` 1 px vertical dimension line from box bottom (48) to the guide (60) with the label
  `12 · inset 60`; the guide is a dashed 1 px line (3 on / 3 off) `#7AA2FF` across the body at y 60.
- The current match's **line top lands on the guide**: B3.4's URL row top is at body top + 60 (match box top + 3).
  A match already in the band [top + 60, bottom] causes no scroll.
- In Source mode the Source field's own scroll applies the same 60 (TD4).

## The design wins: mismatches with the WI body
- F4 draws the current line **42 px** below the body top (line top 87 in a 45-top body). That is inside the 60 px inset and under
  the box's bottom edge. B3.4, the Spec (Req 23) and this WI all say 60; build 60 and treat F4 as a thumbnail.
- WI "B3.4 guide: 10 + 38 + 12": matches the file (box top 10, box 38, clearance 12 = 60).
- WI names B3.8 "12 → 13 live" and B3.9 "after-Esc selection": both match the file. B3.5 is Source mode here (WI 82 lists it as
  no matches: wrong there, right here).
- WI says "Esc lands with the current body match selected": the file shows the selection fill with glyphs in fg0.
