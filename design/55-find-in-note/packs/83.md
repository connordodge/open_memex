## The design wins: mismatches with the WI body
- WI "B3.3 (3 of 12)": B3.3 is no matches; use B1 / B3.10 for `3 of 12`.
- WI says current is "a solid warn fill, radius 2": the file also adds a 1 px outward spread of the same warn colour.
- WI says boxes are "the line-box rect of its range": the file's box is 17 px tall on a 22.95 line (3 px inset top, about 3 bottom),
  not the full line. Take the 17 px from the text's ascent + descent (13.5 px mono is 17.8 natural, so 17 here); check
  against a pumped field. Width is the exact run advance.
- WI does not state the 1 px border is **inside** the fill rect (it is), nor the grid-cell text inset (8.09).
