## WI mismatches and gaps (the design wins)

1. The WI says the chunk's lines are drawn "in the mark highlight". The design draws the chunk's lines on a `surface3` band with `fg0` text and highlights only the typed words in `accentMark`. Passage rows in the list use `accentMark` on the typed words too (pack 76).
2. The WI offers the canvas fallback "if it isn't drawn": it is not drawn, so the fallback applies (title, `<n> cards`, up to 8 card titles).
3. `loading…` / `couldn't load the preview.` are not drawn; style above is a suggestion.
4. Note previews use a serif 26 title, command previews a sans 19 / 600 title; the WI treats both as "title".
5. The "third of the way" scroll is not drawn as a scroll; both boards show unscrolled notes.
6. The `via` meta, the dimmed state, `unavailable`, `NoCli` and the failed-download footer are not drawn; copy follows the WI.
7. The WI's 2-line passage clamp: N1 draws 2 lines, the 400 px column draws one (pack 76, mismatch 2).
8. The wide hint footer for a passage (`show here, at this passage`) is design copy the WI does not list; it belongs to #77's footer, whose passage variant #78 must supply.
9. Some layer names in the Sketch file use an older word for passage text; UI copy says "passage".
