## WI mismatches and gaps (the design wins)

1. Card height: the WI says min(560, h - 48). N1 draws 496 at h 640, which is h - 96 - 48 (top edge 96). Use min(560, h - 96 - 48); the card is top-anchored at 96, not centred.
2. The `↵` chip on the selected row (WI #76) exists only in the narrow card, not beside the preview (pack 76, mismatch 1). The wide hint footer carries `↵` instead.
3. The hint footer is not one card-wide row: in the wide card it is inside the preview slot (so it is gone when the slot is absent), and the narrow card has its own footer with different labels (`choose`, `new tab`).
4. Hint labels depend on the selected kind (`run`, `show here, at this passage`, `choose`). Not drawn: note, daily note and canvas selected in the wide card. Open question for Connor via the lead; fallback `↵ choose`, `⌘↵ new tab`, `esc close` (the N1 wording).
5. `type to search note text` footer: no board draws it (style above is a suggestion).
6. The C3 caption says the label moved from `fg3` to `fg2`; the search icon stays `fg3` at rest and focus (3.46:1, passes as a non-text glyph).
7. Sizes of the 1.3x/1.5x text-scale layouts are not drawn; the WI's overflow test is therefore not backed by a board.
