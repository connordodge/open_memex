## Where the design differs from or adds to the WI (the design wins)
1. **Flash colour is `OmColors.accent` at 12%, not `OmColors.successBg`.** The Sketch fill is `#7CFFB21F` (alpha 31/255 = 12.2%) and the layer is linked to the swatch `successBg`, but `successBg` in code is accent at 10% (`withAlpha(26)`). `EchoArrivalHighlight` already draws accent at `opacity = 0.12`, fading linearly over 1200 ms: use it unchanged. The board shows the highlight at t = 0 (peak).
2. **Corner radius.** The Sketch band has radius 3; `EchoArrivalHighlight` paints a square `drawRect`. Keep the shared widget as is (echo guard tests, ruling D4); rounding is not a test target.
3. **Board 1 is a crop, not a scroll position to copy.** Its drawn editor has the 12 px text-block offset wrapper (`Content` gap −12.1) the port needed to match the browser render, and it draws no heading line for Summer (the Spring paragraph is followed directly by Summer's text at y 114–126). Do not take pixel values from board 1's text block; use the rules and numbers below. The offset wrapper is not part of the design.
4. WI numbers (16 px below the body top) match the file.

## Board 2 geometry: the heading lands 16 px below the body top
- Editor column 832x900: tab strip 36 (divider at y 35), breadcrumb bar 44 (divider at y 79), **body top = y 80**. Body padding-top 16, padding-left 28; text block 776 wide at x 28 (right gap 28). Status bar 28 at the bottom (y 872).
- **Jumped heading line top = y 96 = body top + 16.** The line box is 28 high (19 px mono x 1.47 = 27.93). The heading row itself has 24 px of top padding, so the row's top (y 72) is 8 px above the body top, clipped: this is why the anchor is the heading **line**, not the row.
- Heading text: JetBrains Mono **19, w700, `fg0`**, lh 27.93; the inline wikilink run `the swap` is `accent` (same weight). Text starts at x 36 (8 px inset inside the 776 block).
- **Arrival flash band**: x 28 to 804 (the full 776 text-block width, covering the 8 px inset on both sides), y 96, 776 x 28, `accent` at 12% over the editor field `surface2` `#11151A`. Composite at peak `#1E312C` (30, 49, 44); text sits on it (contrast below). No other rows flash. Fades linearly to nothing over 1200 ms. No caret or selection is drawn by the jump.
- The `#` marker of the jumped heading stays hidden (the drawn heading text has no marker).
- The right panel in board 2 stays on the Outline tab, unscrolled (16 rows fit), with **row 13 `Seeds to order from Hillside Seeds and the swap` current** (accent text, accentSoft tag, 2 px accent bar in guide column 0 at x 42 of the row, no fill) and `Summer` back to a plain row. Row styling is in pack #68.
