# Pack #170 · C3 pointer selection — highlight and newline mark

Boards (page "Option A"): **A4 States** (`20C55F9E-126D-41C3-967B-A10C03BEE1F8`) cell **K** (selection across a paragraph, a heading and list items, design A cell K) and cell **H** (selection across two lines). The flow F1 boards carry no selection.
PNGs: [A4-K](170/a4-k-selection.png) · [A4-H](170/a4-h-selection.png) · [A4-N](170/a4-n-selected-link-ghost.png) · [full A4](../a4-states.png).
Selection colour: pack #159 (`OmColors.selection` = accent at alpha 56, `#7CFFB238`; reads `#28483B` over `surface2`). Real line height 22.95 (Sketch 22.94). Every rect below is relative to its line's top-left; **T** = text column left.

## Board copy (the behaviour)
A4-K: "Drag, Shift+arrow and ⌘A select across paragraphs, headings and item fields as one range. Every line the range touches reveals its markers, the blank line and heading included; lines it does not touch stay rendered. A fully selected line ends in a one-column newline nub. Inside the selection, dimmed markers lift to the secondary text colour so they keep 4.5:1." (secondary text colour = `fg1`.)
A4-H: "Every line the selection touches reveals. Lines outside it stay rendered."

## Target look (decided: the design, no alternatives)
1. **Text slices are tight boxes** at the font's content height (17 px on a 22.95 line, top +2; **21 px on the 34 px H2 line, top +6**), so lines show about 6 px of unhighlighted gap between them. Only the **marker-column fill, the newline mark and a selected blank line are full line height**. "Continuous" = no horizontal break across hidden/revealed markers and rows (adjacent slices touch edge to edge). Do not use Flutter's default line-height-tall selection boxes.
2. Selected link and ghost text lift to `fg1` (board A4-N): [A4-N](170/a4-n-selected-link-ghost.png).

- **Newline mark position:** x = the right end of the line's last glyph (item line: end of the item text, e.g. T + 16.2 + 307.8 for "Hidden markers keep their byte offsets"; revealed paragraph line `[[Byte offsets]].`: right after the `.`). Drawn only at the end of a **logical** line whose newline is inside the selection: not at a soft wrap (first wrapped line of the K paragraph has none), and not on the line where the selection ends mid-line ("Block markers keep|" has none).
- **Selected blank line:** the mark alone, 8.1 x 22.95 at T (K line 2).
- **Wrapped item:** the selection slice covers only the selected characters on each wrapped line (K item 5: slice on line 1 only; line 2 untouched).
- Lines the selection touches reveal: markers `fg2`; markers inside the selection `fg1` (`**`, `[[`, `]]`, `## `, bullet `-`); selected link and ghost text also `fg1`; otherwise unchanged (heading text `fg0` bold, wikilink alias `accent`).
- Lines not touched (K last bullet): painted dot, hidden markers.
- Caret (A4-K / H): drawn at the selection's active end, 2 x 17 `accent`.

## Hit-testing numbers
- Marker column is part of the selection surface but display only (R22). Row gaps: none, so a drag moving straight down crosses row boundaries at multiples of 22.95 within a run of item rows.
- Text cursor over rows (WI/TD5): `SystemMouseCursors.text`.
- Excerpt cards and the Topics footer are outside the text segment: the drag clamps (design silent).
