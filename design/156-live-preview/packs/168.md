## WI vs design (design wins)
1. **Task ring: Sketch = 1.0 px inside, artifact CSS = 1.5 px, WI = 1.5 px.** The approved Sketch file (and its 1x export, measured: stroke about 1.0–1.1 px) draws the unchecked ring at 1 px; the artifact the port came from has `border: 1.5px`. Pack default = the Sketch (1.0, stroke inside the 14 px circle); flip to 1.5 if Connor prefers the artifact. Lead to confirm.
2. **Bullet dots have no painted geometry in the design.** The boards use text glyphs as stand-ins (`•` in JetBrains Mono, `◦` falling back to Menlo, which JetBrains Mono lacks). Numbers below are **measured from the 1x exports** (A1 `•`, A2 `◦`); the implementer may round them.
3. Hit area of the checkbox in the design is the whole marker column (F3 hotspot 32.39 x 22.94), not just the 14 px circle.

## Revealed markers (segment focused, caret or selection touches the item's line)
- The painted marker is replaced (no dot, no circle) by the **source text** left-aligned at colLeft: `fg2`, w400, upright, 13.5, no decoration: `-` (or `*`/`+` as written), `12.`, `>`, `- [ ]`, `- [x]` (the trailing space is part of the column). Leading indent spaces are never drawn (nesting is geometry).
- Widths: bullet 2, numbered unchanged (A4-M `10.` at +0 in a 4-col column), quote 2, **task 4 → 6 columns (32.4 → 48.6)**; text of that row shifts right 16.2 and wraps there; children keep x (parent's *unrevealed* text x). A4-C: revealed `- [ ]` is 40.5 px of text in a 48.6 column.
- Done task revealed (A4-C): marker `- [x]` `fg2`; text keeps line-through `fg2`; caret at the end.
- Display only (R22): the caret never enters the column; a click on the revealed text does nothing.
- **Selected** (selection spans the line): the whole marker column is filled `OmColors.selection` (16.2 x 22.95 for a bullet; full column width, full line height) and the marker text is **`fg1`** `#D6D3CC` (A4-H, A4-K). Unselected revealed markers stay `fg2`.
- Nested caret line (A4-M): the child's column (2 cols) sits at the parent's text x, revealed `-` `fg2`; level-1 parent keeps its dot.
- Off the touched lines, or the segment loses focus: every column returns to painted markers.
- **Read-only (B4 cards, A3, A4-G):** same painted markers and sizes (task circles in a 4-col column), never revealed, toggle disabled; card text base colour `fg1`.
