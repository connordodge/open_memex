## Picker row (`app/lib/src/screens/vault_picker.dart`, after the `VAULT SERVER` `_GridRow`)

- A third `_GridRow`, 8 below the `VAULT SERVER` row, label `BACKUP` (same label style as the other rows: mono 10.5 w500, letterSpacing 0.8, `fg2`, 92 wide, 8 gap).
- Left cell: `KeyedSubtree(key: vault-picker-restore, child: OmButton(kind: secondary, size: 36, icon: LucideIcons.history (14), label: 'restore from backup…'))`. Secondary = `surface3` fill, `line2` border, radius 6, label sans 13 w500 `fg0`.
  Disabled (40%) with the other local actions when the memex binary is not found.
- Right cell: plain text, not a button: "makes a new vault from a .memex.zip; never writes over one" (sans 12, height 1.5, `fg2`, 4 px left inset). Two lines at 1x (36 high). The grid keeps two equal columns.
- Press: `FilePicker.platform.pickFiles` for a `.zip`; cancel does nothing. **Large text:** `_GridRow` is a fixed 36; give the row a minimum height of 36 so the right cell can wrap to 3 lines.

## Mismatches (the design wins)

- The board's own annotation names Keys `restore-choose-folder` and `restore-confirm`. The frozen Keys are `restore-folder` and `restore-submit`; use the frozen ones.
- R4 to R7 omit the meta line ("41.2 MB · made …") and R3 omits the hint line; the WI and requirement 35 say the dialog shows the file's name, size and date. Implement as drawn (error replaces the target block); lead to rule whether the meta line should stay in error states.
- The meta line shows the file name's UTC timestamp as written (14:03 for 140312Z); the board does not show a time-zone conversion. Lead to confirm UTC versus local.
