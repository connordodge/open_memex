## The three states (one table, caret moves through the flow)
| state (board) | caret / selection | what shows | row's layout box |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rendered** (T1.1 caret on the plain line above; T1.3 caret at the end of the plain line below) | not on any table line, or segment unfocused | the grid (design B: band, rules, aligned cells); the field is mounted but offstage; no selection paint (R16) | the **grid box**: x from text-column left T, width W, height = band + body rows (pack #203; 122.75 for header + 4 one-line rows) |
| **Revealed** (T1.2 caret after `Marker pars`; B3-A caret after `Caret mapping`; B3-I selection) | caret or selection touches ANY line of the table, segment focused | the field shows every table line raw (below); the grid, band and rules are gone | the **field box**: x from T, width W (no code-row insets), height = sum of its wrapped lines x 22.95 |
| **Rendered again** (T1.3) | caret left the table, or focus left the segment | grid back; selection kept but not painted | grid box |
The caret placed by the click in T1.1 to T1.2 is at the SAME character: `| **Marker pars|er** ...` (cell text x 90 px from the cell content left is char 11; raw offset = 2 `| ` + 2 `**` + 11 = 15 in that line).
Touch rule = #157 R2 (same as prose): caret at a table line's start, inside it or at its content end touches it; a caret at the start of the line AFTER the table does not (T1.3 caret is on the next line, table stays rendered); a selection that reaches any table line reveals the **whole** table (B3-I: selection from the line above through the header's second cell reveals all of it); Cmd+A reveals every table in the segment.

## What the grid occupies, what the field occupies
- Grid and field both start at the row top, x = T (daily note: field left + 14 gutter = 194 in B1, 42 in B2; topic note T = field left) and span W (B1 746, B2 650, B3 snippet 602, card 578). Neither has side insets: the grid's cell padding lives inside it.
- Rendered height `Hr = k_h*L + H + sum(body rows)`; revealed height `Hv = (2 + N)*L` for N one-line body rows with no wrapped raw line (a raw line longer than W wraps like any prose line and adds whole 22.95 lines, B3-G revealed).
- **The step = Hv - Hr = L - H = 22.95 - 8 = 14.95**, for every N (0, 1, 4: 45.9 - 30.95; 137.7 - 122.75 = 14.95; board J: "RENDERED 122.75 PX" / "REVEALED 137.70 PX"). The delimiter line is the only line that changes: hidden it is the 8 px absorbed in the header band (split 4 above and 4 below the header text), revealed it is a full 22.95 line. Everything above the table keeps its y; everything below moves down by 14.95 on reveal and back on unreveal (same arithmetic as a hidden vs revealed code fence). The header text itself moves from band top + 4 to band top + 0, and body row r's text from `30.95 + r*22.95` to `45.9 + r*22.95` (the +14.95 shift).
- The flow boards show 14.86 (452.88 - 438.02) because Sketch lines are 22.94; use 14.95.
- Scaled text: the step is `scaledL - scaledH` (27.54 - 9.6 = 17.94 at 1.2x). The T3 stub's own height is unconstrained by the design (T4 fills the body); only the seam behaviour is asserted.

## Revealed field (what T5 styles; shown here for the seam's line map)
Every table line is one 22.95 line: header line `| Step | Notes | Est. h | State |` (pipes and their spaces `fg2` `#9B9A94`, cell text bold `fg0`), delimiter line `|:--|:--|--:|:-:|` (whole line `fg2`, regular), body lines raw with inline markers dimmed `fg2`. The raw line text equals the source line, so offsets are the field's. Typing, popover, undo as prose (R13).

## Selection while rendered (R16) and on reveal
- Rendered with the segment selection touching the table: the grid draws NO `OmColors.selection` (`#7CFFB238`); the selection is only stored. Refocus reveals it with that selection intact (B3-I draws it: fill on the selected slices, markers lifted to `fg1`, pack #159 numbers).
- B3-I shows the selection from `for the retro:` (line above), the blank line (1 mono column wide newline mark) and `| Step | Notes` of the header line; the table is revealed.

## States the logic must reproduce (one-line each, from the boards)
A caret on a header, delimiter or body line reveals (A, T1.2) · caret on the plain line above/below keeps the grid (T1.1, T1.3) · selection across the table edge reveals (I) · typing the last `|` of a delimiter under a header turns the lines into a table (R10; design silent, the table appears rendered unless the caret is in it, which it is) · a row with fewer cells reveals as its short raw line (D) · more cells than the header: grid drops them, field shows them (D).
