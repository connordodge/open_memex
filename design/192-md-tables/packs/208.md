## Display only (R25)
No reveal, no caret, no selection, no hover or Cmd styles (T2.2's underline never shows), no pointer cursor, no recognizers or `MouseRegion` (D14). The grid, wikilinks and links are painted, not interactive. Cards keep their own caption/x gestures (pack #172). Markers always hidden (`forceHidden`), no inline marker ever shows (`**`, `` ` ``, `[[`).

## Several tables, joined paragraph (D23; design silent, behaviour from the Spec)
Two consecutive tables (the second's header + delimiter right after the first's last body line) render as two grids stacked in the same card row flow with no extra gap (design draws one table per card; suggest 0: the first's last row has no rule and the second's band starts directly below). A pipe paragraph directly after a table in an excerpt (blocks joined with one `\n`) renders as a further body row of that table (22.95, aligned by the table's columns, accepted artifact).

## Keys and semantics
Key `card-table-<r>` (r = the card row index) on each grid. Semantics as pack #205 (header cells, then rows, left to right).
