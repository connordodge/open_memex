## Where the WI differs from the design
- #363 says an optional `actionKey` on `PanelHeader` is enough. The design needs more: a 57x22 hit box (padding 3/6, radius 4), R8.5 header padding for VAULTS only, and the pressed (held while the modal shows) and focus-ring states. `PanelHeader` renders a bare `Text` today. Either extend `PanelHeader` (`actionKey`, an action button with focus and `pressed`) or draw this header in `sidebar.dart`; CANVASES must stay as is.
- Pill menu-open fill (`surface3` + `line3`) is not in the WI.
- Menu items' per-icon Lucide names (folderSearch, x, trash2, settings2) are not in the WI; they match `folder_menu.dart` conventions (`trash2`, `settings2`).
