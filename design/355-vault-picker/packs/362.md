## Where the WI differs from the design
- **Dialog width**: the WI says 640 for A1 and A2. A1 is 640; **A2 is drawn 672** (window minus 48). At 720 a 640 cap would leave 40px gutters; use `min(640, window - 48)` unless Connor wants 672 at narrow widths. Flag to the lead.
- Column: the WI says a 560 column; inside the modal it is 558 (border 1 + padding 40). Use 40 padding inside the border and let the column fill.
- Dialog fill is `surface1` (the delete dialog is `surface2`).
- The `esc` chip's Menlo 10 is the board's stand-in for the app's mono kbd style (same as the `⌘K` chip on the boards); use `OmKbd` inside a 1px `line2` box.
