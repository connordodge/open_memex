## WI vs design (design wins)
1. **`docs/hosted-vault.md` is not monospace and has no copy button in the design.** Step 1 and the footer draw it as a link: Inter Tight, `info` colour (no underline). The WI's coordinator ruling asks for monospace text plus a copy button on the footer line (`setup-copy-docs`). This is a contradiction. Pack default = **the board** (info-coloured Inter Tight text, no copy button, tapping navigates nowhere). If the lead keeps the ruling, add a 24x24 `copy` ibtn (12 px `fg2`) after the footer path and render the path in JetBrains Mono 12 `info`. Lead to decide with Connor.
2. Copy buttons on steps 2, 3, 4 only: matches the board. Commands and example values match the WI.
3. The sheet is **flush with the top of the window content** and its top corners are square; only the bottom corners are round (radius 0, 0, 10, 10). The border is drawn on the left, right and bottom (1 px `line2`); the top edge sits on the title-bar hairline. In Flutter: `Align(topCenter)` with no top margin, `RoundedRectangleBorder(borderRadius: BorderRadius.vertical(bottom: Radius.circular(10)))`, side 1 `line2` (a 1 px top line, if the shape draws one, is invisible against the title bar and acceptable).
4. Scroll: the board needs 615 px; the WI requires a 720x900 window to fit and the sheet to scroll when it does not. Derived: sheet `maxHeight = window height`, body scrolls, eyebrow row and footer row stay pinned (not drawn).
