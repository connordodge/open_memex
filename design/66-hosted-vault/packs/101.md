- **Window title.** The boards draw "memex · nas" in the title bar for a hosted vault. The WI and coordinator ruling keep the native title unchanged. Nothing to build here; flagged for the lead. No status dot in the title bar (matches the WI).
- **Vault-gone banner is not drawn.** The WI asks this pack to decide. Decision (derived from the two drawn banners): danger styling as state 2, leading `LucideIcons.folderX` 14 px `danger`, text "<vault> is no longer on <server>" in `danger`, **no button** (the picker opens at once; #96 owns that). - Banner buttons are `OmButton` small (24 px, 12 px label, padding 10): see the small-button gap in [pack 69](69.md).
